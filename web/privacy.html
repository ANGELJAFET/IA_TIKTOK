Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Fact Drop Studio ("the App") is a personal publishing tool operated by its owner. This policy explains what data the App handles.

Data we access

When the account owner authorizes the App through TikTok Login Kit, the App receives an access token, a refresh token and the account's open ID, plus basic profile information (user.info.basic) needed to show the account name and posting options. The App uses the video.publish and video.upload permissions only to upload videos created by the owner to the owner's own TikTok account.

How data is stored

Tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private deployment environment and are never sold, shared or used for advertising. The App does not collect data from TikTok viewers or other users.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the integration is active. The owner can revoke access at any time from TikTok settings (Security and permissions → Apps), after which the stored tokens stop working and are deleted.
